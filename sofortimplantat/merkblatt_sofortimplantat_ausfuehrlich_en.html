Immediate Implant
Immediate Implant
With an immediate implant, a non-restorable tooth is removed and the implant may be placed in the fresh extraction socket during the same appointment. This is only done when the conditions are safe.

Requirements

Important requirements include an intact bone wall, controlled inflammation, sufficient primary stability, suitable soft tissue and a provisional restoration that does not load the implant.

Plan B is part of the plan

If a requirement is missing, the implant is not forced. The tooth is removed, the socket may be stabilized and implant placement is planned after healing.

Procedure

The tooth is removed carefully. The socket, bone wall and inflammation are assessed directly. Only then is the final decision made.

Visible gap / provisional restoration

In the aesthetic zone, the provisional restoration must be prepared by your general dentist before surgery and must not load the fresh implant.

Typical timeline

Planning

OPG/DVT, findings, provisional restoration and costs.

Surgery day

Extraction and assessment of the socket.

Implant or plan change

Immediate implant only if primary stability is safe.

Healing

Usually 3-4 months without loading.

Final crown

After clearance, usually by your general dentist.

Aftercare

  • Do not bite on the implant or provisional restoration.
  • Cool gently and rest for 48 hours.
  • Avoid smoking.
  • Attend controls and suture removal after 1-2 weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Can every tooth be replaced immediately?

No. Bone wall, inflammation and implant stability decide.

Will I receive an immediate fixed crown?

Usually no. A temporary aesthetic solution is possible but must be free of load.

What endangers success?

Smoking, poor hygiene, uncontrolled diabetes, strong inflammation and loading of the provisional restoration.
