Hyaluronic acid for skin, lines and lips

Hyaluronic acid treatments serve different purposes: improving selected aspects of skin quality, softening suitable folds or changing lip contour and volume. We select the approach according to your findings and wishes.

What hyaluronic acid is

Hyaluronic acid binds water and occurs naturally in the body. Specially manufactured gels are used for injection. Their properties differ in cross-linking, firmness and distribution within tissue. Being related to a natural substance does not make an injected product risk-free.

Skin quality and volume are different goals

Skinboosters

Usually softer gels are distributed in small amounts within the skin. Depending on the product, the goal is improved hydration, smoothness or elasticity. Major volume enhancement is not the main aim.

Contour and folds

A gel suitable for the region is used to soften selected folds or change shape and volume. Its consistency must match the treatment goal.

Skinbooster is not a single product standard. Not every fluid hyaluronic acid product is intended for every skin layer or facial area. Some products are stabilised or cross-linked, while others have different properties. The treatment cycle and expected duration depend on the selected product.

What we plan together

We assess skin, tissue support, existing asymmetry and previous treatment. We then agree the area, product, amount and a realistic goal. A staged approach may be appropriate. More filler does not automatically mean a better result.

Treatment is voluntary. Choosing no treatment is reasonable. Marked excess skin or tissue descent cannot be corrected indefinitely by adding volume.

Nasolabial folds

These lines run from the sides of the nose towards the corners of the mouth. Their appearance depends on skin, tissue support and facial anatomy as well as the fold itself. We assess whether targeted correction is appropriate and what change would suit your face.

Completely filling away a fold is not always possible or desirable. Blood vessels in this region require particular attention. Using a cannula does not eliminate vascular risk.

Marionette lines

These lines extend from the mouth corners towards the chin. In suitable cases, hyaluronic acid may support contour correction. More pronounced tissue descent or excess skin limits what is achievable; other approaches may be more appropriate.

Lip contour and augmentation

Depending on your wishes, contour, proportions or volume may be changed. Existing asymmetry is discussed. Lip function must be considered; a particular photograph or exactly matching shape cannot be promised.

Lips may initially be noticeably swollen and tender. Their immediate appearance is therefore not a reliable final result. If you have a history of cold sores, preventive treatment may be discussed.

Skinboosters

The focus is skin quality. Small injections may temporarily leave visible raised areas or bruising. Depending on the product, a series of sessions followed by assessment may be planned. No single schedule applies to all products.

Alternatives

Skin care and sun protection are useful regardless of injections. Depending on your concern, skin resurfacing, separately planned botulinum toxin for expression-related lines or surgery may be options. Benefits, effort and risks differ. Not every line needs treatment.

This information covers hyaluronic acid for skin quality, suitable folds and lips. Nose reshaping, tear troughs and other specialised areas are not assumed to be part of this plan.

Preparation

  • Tell us about previous fillers, including product, region and date, even if treatment was long ago. Unknown or permanent materials require particular assessment.
  • Disclose allergies, particularly to local anaesthetics, previous reactions to hyaluronidase, cold sores, immunosuppression, autoimmune disease and bleeding disorders.
  • List all medication and do not stop it yourself. Discuss infections, inflamed skin, dental infections and planned dental or skin procedures beforehand.
  • Active infection requires postponement. Elective treatment is avoided during pregnancy and breastfeeding. This information is intended for adults.

Procedure and local anaesthesia

After examination and consent, the skin is cleaned and disinfected. A suitable gel is injected into the planned area with a needle or cannula. We assess the shape and tissue during treatment. Neither needle nor cannula eliminates vascular complications.

Some products contain a local anaesthetic. Anaesthetic cream or additional local anaesthesia may help, particularly for lips. Pressure, burning or pain may still occur. Sedation or general anaesthesia is normally unnecessary. Anaesthetics have their own risks, including allergy and temporary numbness.

Possible adverse effects

  • Swelling, redness, pain, tenderness, bruising, itching and temporary unevenness.
  • Asymmetry, over- or under-correction, visible or palpable gel, a bluish tint or migration of material.
  • Infection, cold-sore reactivation, nodules or delayed inflammatory reactions. These may first appear weeks or months later and require treatment.
  • Allergy, rarely severe. Correction or dissolution can also involve further risks and costs.
Rare but especially important: gel entering a vessel or impairment of blood supply may cause tissue death and permanent scars. Very rarely, visual disturbance including blindness or a stroke can occur. This risk also applies in principle to lips and skinboosters.

Can hyaluronic acid be dissolved?

Hyaluronidase can support breakdown in appropriate situations; its use may be off-label. Allergy and other adverse effects are possible. Complete dissolution, restoration of the original appearance or prevention of permanent damage cannot be guaranteed.

Progress and duration

Early appearance is affected by swelling. Once it settles, often after about two weeks, the result is assessed. For skinboosters, assessment may be more meaningful after the agreed treatment cycle.

How long a change remains visible depends on product, area, metabolism and treatment. An effect lasting several months is possible, but no uniform duration can be promised. Gel may remain in tissue longer than the visible effect. Further treatment is decided individually.

Aftercare

Avoid strenuous exercise, strong heat and alcohol on the treatment day. Do not massage or apply firm pressure yourself. Continue to avoid strong heat and intense sun while redness or swelling persists. Apply make-up only after puncture sites have closed, following our advice.

After lip anaesthesia, wait until normal sensation returns before eating or having hot drinks. If needed, cool gently with breaks and without pressure. Use pain relief or cold-sore prevention only according to personal advice. Coordinate planned dental and skin procedures beforehand.

Possible impaired circulation: unusual severe or increasing pain, white, grey-blue, mottled or cold skin: call immediately and have the area examined. Do not wait until the following day.

Costs and private payment

Costs are agreed individually as a privately paid service, according to the region, product, amount, treatment cycle and follow-up. Possible further sessions or corrections are discussed in advance. Health-insurance reimbursement is not guaranteed.
