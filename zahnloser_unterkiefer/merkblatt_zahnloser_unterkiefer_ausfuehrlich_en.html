Edentulous Lower Jaw
Implants in an edentulous lower jaw often aim to stabilize a lower denture. Even two implants can significantly improve denture retention in suitable cases.

Possible treatment goals

Typical options include two implants for a removable overdenture, more implants for a bar solution, or a fixed restoration in selected cases.

Why the lower jaw is special

The mandibular nerve limits many posterior positions. Implants are therefore often planned in the anterior lower jaw between the nerve exits.

The denture remains part of treatment

The existing denture often needs relief, soft lining or later incorporation of locator or bar attachments.

Aftercare

  • Use the denture only as instructed and pressure-free.
  • Do not bite on fresh implants.
  • Attend pressure-point controls and prosthetic adjustments.

Frequently asked questions

Are two implants enough?

Often yes for stabilizing a removable lower denture. Other goals may require more implants.

Why not simply place implants in the posterior jaw?

Nerve position and bone height often limit posterior placement.

Are immediate fixed teeth possible?

Only if stability and the prosthetic concept are suitable.
