Example digital visualisation of a fixed lower jaw restoration on 8 implants. A fixed restoration requires a different implant distribution than a removable denture, precise prosthetic planning and sufficient cleanability. Whether this concept is suitable is planned individually according to jaw shape, nerve anatomy, bite situation, patient handling and cost framework.
