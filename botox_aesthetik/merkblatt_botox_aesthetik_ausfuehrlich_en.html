Aesthetic treatment with botulinum toxin

Treatment may temporarily reduce expression-related facial lines. Planning focuses on your facial movement, anatomy and an agreed, realistic goal.

How botulinum toxin works

Botulinum toxin type A temporarily reduces activation of selected facial muscles. The skin in those areas is drawn together less strongly. The medicine does not add volume or change the basic structure of the skin.

Areas commonly discussed

Frown lines

Vertical lines between the eyebrows can become more noticeable with concentration or tension.

Forehead

Horizontal lines appear when the eyebrows rise. Brow position and function must be considered in planning.

Outer eye lines

Fine lines beside the eyes appear when smiling. Eye function and eyelid closure remain important.

Other areas

Uses outside the selected product’s approval need a separate assessment and, where applicable, explicit off-label consent.

Approval depends on the selected medicine, area, age and other conditions. Not every use commonly described as “Botox” has the same regulatory status.

What treatment cannot reliably change

Marked excess skin, loss of volume and deeply established lines at rest may remain. More medicine does not automatically produce a better result. Complete symmetry or elimination of all lines cannot be guaranteed.

Treatment is voluntary. We discuss your wishes without pressure. Preserving natural expression and some individual lines can form part of the agreed goal.

Consultation and preparation

We assess your face at rest and in motion, existing asymmetry, eyelid and brow position, and previous treatments. Tell us about medication, allergies, neuromuscular conditions, swallowing or breathing problems and previous botulinum toxin injections, including the dates and product.

Some antibiotics and medicines affecting muscles can interact. Do not stop blood thinners yourself. Active inflammation in the treatment area or other reasons for unsuitability prevent treatment. Elective aesthetic treatment is postponed during pregnancy and breastfeeding. This information is intended for adults.

Procedure and anaesthesia

  1. Agree the goal and areas. We discuss what change appears appropriate and achievable.
  2. Give targeted injections. After disinfection, small amounts are injected into the selected muscles using fine needles. Amount and distribution are chosen individually.
  3. Assess progress. Results are not immediate. We arrange a review and decide on further steps if necessary.

The injections may briefly sting or hurt. Sedation or general anaesthesia is normally unnecessary. Cooling or suitable local measures can be discussed if needed. You remain awake.

Possible risks

  • Redness, swelling, bruising, injection pain, headache, infection or allergy.
  • Unintended excessive or uneven muscle relaxation, causing asymmetry, a heavy forehead sensation or a drooping brow or upper eyelid.
  • Depending on the area, dry eyes, impaired eyelid closure, double vision or a changed smile. Noticeable eye symptoms need prompt medical assessment.
  • Rarely, marked general muscle weakness and speech, swallowing or breathing problems from effects beyond the target area. Severe allergies are rare but possible.
  • Insufficient or shorter-than-desired effect; frequent treatments may encourage antibody formation and reduced response.
Botulinum toxin cannot simply be dissolved immediately after injection. Adverse effects may need medical treatment and often improve as the muscle effect wears off.

Onset and duration

Initial changes usually appear after several days and develop over the following weeks. A review often takes place after about two weeks; the maximum effect may occur later, depending on the product and area.

The effect often lasts about three to four months, but duration and strength vary. Repeat treatment depends on progress and the product-specific minimum interval; it is not automatic.

After treatment

Do not firmly rub or massage injection sites on the treatment day. Initially avoid strenuous exercise, strong heat and alcohol. Follow personal advice if redness or swelling occurs. Do not drive if vision or other relevant functions are affected.

Contact us for increasing inflammation, noticeable asymmetry or eye and eyelid symptoms. Do not attempt to correct the result yourself or arrange closely spaced injections at multiple providers.

New speech, swallowing or breathing problems or marked general muscle weakness require immediate medical assessment.

Alternatives and costs

Choosing no treatment is always an option. Depending on the cause, skin care and sun protection, skin resurfacing, separately planned hyaluronic acid treatment or surgery may be considered. No single combination suits every face.

Costs are agreed individually as a privately paid service. Areas, extent, review and possible further sessions are discussed beforehand. Health-insurance reimbursement is not guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

Is botulinum toxin the same as hyaluronic acid?

No. Botulinum toxin affects muscle activity. Depending on the product, hyaluronic acid is used for skin quality, volume or contour. The procedures have different risks.

Can I decide later?

Yes. Take time to decide. A consultation does not commit you to treatment.

Your checklist

Goal and limits clear? All previous treatments disclosed? Approval or off-label use discussed? Risks, private costs and review appointment agreed?
