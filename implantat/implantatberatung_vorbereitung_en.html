Preparing for an Implant Consultation
Before the first consultation it is often not yet clear whether a simple implant, bone augmentation, sinus lift or a larger reconstruction will be needed. This page gives orientation before the individual treatment plan is made.

What we check at the appointment

We assess the missing tooth or teeth, the available bone, gum conditions, neighbouring structures, medical risk factors and the prosthetic goal.

The exact pathway is decided after examination and, if needed, 3D imaging.

Possible treatment pathways

The options range from a simple implant without augmentation to simultaneous minor augmentation, internal or external sinus lift, shell technique, semilunar technique, membrane-supported GBR or a non-implant alternative.

Large vertical defects often require staged treatment: first bone augmentation, later implant placement after sufficient healing.

Why not all detailed documents at once?

Many detailed information sheets repeat the same basic content and some may not apply to your case. After the consultation you receive the document that matches the actual plan.

What to bring

Please bring your medication list, allergies, existing X-rays or 3D scans, and any denture or provisional restoration. Please tell us about smoking, diabetes, blood thinners, bisphosphonates/denosumab and immune suppression.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know the technique before the appointment?

No. Choosing the technique is part of the consultation and planning.

What if there is not enough bone?

Depending on the region, options include sinus lift, bone augmentation, shell technique, membrane-supported GBR or a prosthetic alternative.

Is 3D imaging always private?

DVT/CBCT is usually a private diagnostic service and is discussed before it is performed.
