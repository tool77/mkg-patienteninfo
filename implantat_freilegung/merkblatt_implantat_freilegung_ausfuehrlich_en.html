Implant Uncovering
Implant uncovering is the transition from healing to prosthetic restoration. After placement, the implant has usually healed underneath the gum. During uncovering, it is made accessible again so the gum can be prepared for the abutment, impression or scan, and later crown or denture.

Why is uncovering needed?

The implant is initially not visible

Many implants heal protected underneath the gum. This reduces loading during the early healing phase. After sufficient healing time, the implant must be reachable again for the next treatment step.

Shaping the gum

During uncovering, a healing cap or gingiva former is usually attached. This component shapes the later passage through the gum and prepares the prosthetic restoration.

Typical sequence

1 · Check after healing

We check whether the implant has healed stably and whether uncovering can be performed appropriately.

2 · Uncovering short procedure

The gum is opened locally and the implant is fitted with a healing cap or gingiva former.

3 · Soft tissue usually about 4 weeks

The gum heals around the healing cap and forms the later emergence profile of the restoration.

4 · Prosthetics general dentist

After clearance, impression or scan follows, then the restoration with abutment, crown, bridge or denture.

What does this mean for you?

Usually well tolerated

Uncovering is usually much smaller than implant placement. It is usually performed under local anaesthesia.

Coordination with your dentist

After clearance, the prosthetic phase begins. We coordinate timing so your dentist can continue with impression or scan.

Do not manipulate

The healing cap must not be loosened or unscrewed by yourself. Please contact us if it loosens, causes pressure or becomes painful.

Risks and aftercare

Possible symptoms

Mild postoperative bleeding, swelling, pressure, delayed wound healing, infection or a soft tissue problem can occur. Rarely, the gum may need an additional correction later.

After the procedure
  • Keep the area clean but do not traumatise it.
  • Do not manipulate the healing cap.
  • Contact us if loosening, increasing swelling, pus or postoperative bleeding occurs.
  • Coordinate the appointment for prosthetic continuation.
Key point: Implant uncovering does not place the tooth. It makes the healed implant accessible and shapes the gum so abutment, impression or scan, and the prosthetic restoration can proceed as planned.
